What this chapter is about

Lenses and mirrors, put to work. You'll see how a magnifying glass, a compound microscope and a telescope make things look bigger, and what limits the detail they can show. Then the chapter turns to the spectrometer, measuring the speed of light, and the optical fibres that carry the internet.
