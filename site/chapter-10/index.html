Optical Instruments

What this chapter is about

In this chapter, some optical instruments that are based on the principles of reflection and refraction will be discussed. The most common of these instruments are the magnifying glass, compound microscope and telescopes. We shall also study magnification and resolving powers of these optical instruments. The spectrometer and an arrangement for measurement of the speed of light are also described. An introduction to optical fibres, which has developed a great importance in medical diagnostics, telecommunication and computer networking, is also included.
